## Post copy

Most "speed to lead" advice is built on a study from 2007. Your buyers live in 2026.

I went back through the current research and rebuilt the playbook for teams working real-time internet leads — mortgage, insurance, solar, education. A few things that should change how you staff and run the team:

The phone went quiet. About 86% of calls from unknown numbers now go unanswered. Speed to lead still wins — but speed now means an instant, branded text and a way to self-book, not a faster dial.

Text passed the phone. In 2026, consumers name text (46%) over a call (43%) as their preferred first contact. Lead with a text to earn the conversation. For high-stakes money decisions, the call is still how you close: text to engage, voice to close.

The job moved from informing to validating. Leads now arrive already researched, often by AI — and 69% of buyers use a human to validate what the AI told them. Staff for consultative conversations, not for dialing.

Right-size the pipeline. Even one lead a day compounds fast. Around 20 active leads a month per rep is usually plenty; cap the queue near 100 before conversion degrades.

And compliance isn't a footnote in our world. Financial services and insurance make up roughly 47% of all TCPA lawsuits. Build consent into the form so you can move fast without exposure.

Full research, the charts, and a downloadable playbook are here:
https://howtoworkleads.com/sales-process/how-to-build-a-real-time-internet-lead-team
